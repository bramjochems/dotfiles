# Feature ideas

The owner wants a short list to choose from, not a brainstorm. What makes an idea worth the time is
that it comes from the project's own evidence and says what it does for the customer.

## Read first

Find these through the project's CLAUDE.md, or its `docs/agentic-workflow.bindings.md` when it has one:
the product's purpose and stage, the roadmap and STATUS (what is live, for whom), the backlog (ideas
already parked, with their triggers), the review findings log when there is one (recurring problems),
and anything recording customer feedback or usage. If the owner named an area, read that area's code
and plan. Don't propose what the roadmap or backlog already has; point to the row instead, and say if
its trigger now looks met.

## The answer

Four to six ideas, best first by the effect for the customer at the product's current stage. For each:

- **The idea**, in one line, in the domain's words.
- **What it does for whom:** the customer problem it solves, and the evidence for that problem
  (a backlog row, a finding, feedback, a usage fact). Mark an idea that rests on no evidence as such.
- **Cost:** rough size (a slice, a wave), and what it touches that is hard to undo.
- **What it rests on:** the assumption that would make it worthless if wrong.

Then one line: which you would do first and why. Ideas the evidence argues against go in one closing
line, not in the list. Nothing is built or filed until the owner picks.
